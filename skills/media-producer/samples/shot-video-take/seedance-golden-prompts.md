# Seedance Golden Prompt Samples

Use these as shape examples. Adapt to actual take context, provider preview,
and user direction. Do not copy names, places, or actions blindly.

## Text-Only Single Shot

```text
One continuous six-second establishing shot of a rain-dark market arcade at
night, empty except for wind pushing hanging cloth signs. Camera: slow low
dolly forward under the stone arches, ending on a closed wooden door with warm
light leaking under it. Sound: soft rain, distant crowd murmur, cloth snapping
once in the wind. Look and continuity: muted green and amber palette, wet stone,
handheld naturalism, no modern signage. Do not include subtitles, logos,
vehicles, electric lights, or extra characters.
```

## Image-To-Video / First Frame

```text
@Image1 is the opening frame. Begin from its exact composition, character

```

## First-And-Last Frame

```text
@Image1 is the first frame. Start from its exact subject identity, composition,
market doorway layout, light direction, torn petition, and wardrobe.

@Image2 is the required final frame. End at its closer composition with the
petition raised into the doorway light.

Transition: Elise steps half a pace forward and raises the petition through
real body and arm movement. Keep identity, prop design, crowd placement, and
doorway geography continuous. The change should happen through one restrained
handheld push-in, not morphing. Sound: crowd ambience drops as the paper catches
light. Do not include subtitles, new props, extra characters, changed wardrobe,
or architecture morphing.
```

Read @Image1 as ordered physical waypoints in one uninterrupted camera path, not
as a first frame and not as a page layout to copy. Do not blend panels into one

storyboard page.

STORYBOARD PANELS AS VIDEO BEATS
Waypoint 1 / upper left: the aerial camera begins outside the wall, high over

```text

angle, shot scale, action rhythm, and screen direction only. @Image2 is only the
character wardrobe and face reference. @Image3 is only the location material and
scale reference.

CRITICAL STORYBOARD RULE
Turn the storyboard panels into an edited cinematic sequence in order. Do not
render sketch lines, panel borders, arrows, captions, labels, page layout, or
text from @Image1.

Shot 1 / Panel 1: low wide shot, the runner enters frame left between shelves,
body leaning forward, camera tracking backward.
Shot 2 / Panel 2: medium side angle, the runner pivots around the table,
camera arcs with the turn.

breath visible, background streaking slightly.
Shot 4 / Panel 4: over-shoulder reveal through the opened door, motion resolves
into a steady final frame.

LOOK AND RENDER TRANSLATION
Render as live-action cinematic footage using @Image2 and @Image3 for final
appearance. Do not render hand-drawn storyboard texture or notation.
```
