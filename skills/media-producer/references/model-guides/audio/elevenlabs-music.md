# Eleven Music Prompt Guide

Describe the intended composition through musical form, instrumentation, mood,
tempo or energy, vocal presence, production character, and narrative timing.

- State whether the result is instrumental or includes vocals.
- Describe structural development rather than listing disconnected genre tags.
- Tie changes in intensity or instrumentation to known story beats when music
  accompanies a scene.
- Do not request copyrighted lyrics or imitate a living artist by name; describe
  the desired musical traits directly.
- Keep duration and instrumental controls in provider request fields when the
  live schema exposes them.

## Provenance

Recovered from the ElevenLabs music route guidance introduced with the provider
