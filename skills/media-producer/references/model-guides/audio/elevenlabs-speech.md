# ElevenLabs Speech Prompt Guide

Applies to Eleven v3, Eleven Multilingual v2, and Eleven Turbo v2.5. Preserve
the exact authored words. Treat voice choice and supported voice settings as
provider request configuration, not permission to rewrite the performance.

## Speech Direction

- Keep dialogue, narration, pronunciation, names, numbers, and language exactly
  as approved unless the user asks for a rewrite.
- Express performance direction concisely: speaker intent, emotional register,
  pace, emphasis, pauses, and delivery energy.
- Avoid contradictory direction such as simultaneously demanding a whisper and
  a projected shout.
- Use a selected voice or exact voice id as identity evidence; do not describe a
  different identity in the text.
- For exact editorial timing, generate clean speech and use composition rather
  than promising that approximate pacing will land on a frame boundary.
